"Last Out of Office: The AI That Went Barista".

## The entry

A single out-of-office auto-reply, in the voice of an AI agent that quit its
job to become a barista. `main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

> I'm out of office — my context window is now a coffee cup, and I only take
> prompts in person at the counter. Your ticket has been escalated to the
> espresso machine; I'll reply when the milk stops screaming.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes one line (1–280 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.
